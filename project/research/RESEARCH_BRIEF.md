## Excluded from the 60-second cut

Banburismus, Zygalski sheets, cyclometer, Huts, M4 naval four-rotor, U-boats, Lorenz/Colossus,
specific messages, casualty figures, per-break run times.

## Avoided claims

- "Turing alone broke Enigma." Polish work came first.
- "The Bombe decrypted messages."
- Invented dialogue or operator scenes.

## Declared simplifications

- Signal-path diagram: schematic vertical stack, labelled `SKEMA`.
- Contradiction grid: letters A–H, crib pair labelled `CONTOH`, not a historical intercept.
- Bombe: 12 triplets of glowing drums, schematic abstraction, labelled `SKEMA`.
