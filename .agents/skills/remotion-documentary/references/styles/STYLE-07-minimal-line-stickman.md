# STYLE-07 — Minimal Line / Stickman

## Reference reading
The examples use a black or near-black field with a very simple white line drawing of a bus and tiny line-figure people. One reference also shows a gold/yellow illuminated outline figure in a dark space; that glowing treatment belongs primarily to STYLE-03. This style is the unlit minimal line version: sparse, monochrome and diagrammatic.

## Visual signature
- Black/dark background or a deliberately plain high-contrast field.
- Thin white/off-white line drawing with very little fill.
- Extremely simplified human figures and objects, readable by outline and pose.
- Large negative space; only essential scene elements are drawn.
- Bus, room, floor or props represented with a few confident contours.
- Minimalism is intentional: do not add detail simply to make the frame look fuller.

## Geometry and line treatment
- Lock stroke width, cap/join style, head size, limb proportions and object line density.
- Use simple geometric construction: circles, straight/curved lines and sparse contours.
- People are stick figures or minimal line figures, not detailed illustrated humans.
- Distinguish characters through pose, position and one small prop rather than elaborate clothing.
- Avoid fills unless required to clarify overlap or focus.

## Palette and lighting
- Core palette: black/near-black background and white/off-white strokes.
- Optional single accent color only if approved; if the accent glows, treat it as a deliberate hybrid with STYLE-03.
- No realistic lighting or material shading. Contrast comes from line and negative space.

## Camera and layout
- Use front/side diagrammatic views and simple stage-like compositions.
- Keep the action centered or aligned to a clear baseline/floor line.
- Use empty space to direct attention; avoid decorative background objects.
- Camera usually remains static, with simple pans or zooms only when needed.

## Motion language
- Progressive line drawing, line erasure, simple pose swaps, walking cycles, pointer movement and object reveal.
- Use clean timing and readable holds. A small movement can carry the whole shot.
- Do not use glow, bloom or complex particles unless the user explicitly approves a Glow Sketch hybrid.

## Asset and prompt requirements
Specify background, stroke HEX/width, exact figure proportions, pose, object outline and whether isolated transparent line art is required. One figure per character asset. Request no shading, no color fills, no text and no extra scene clutter.

## Do not
Do not turn this into detailed documentary stickman, comic art, whiteboard chalk, neon glow, realistic human illustration or a dense infographic.
