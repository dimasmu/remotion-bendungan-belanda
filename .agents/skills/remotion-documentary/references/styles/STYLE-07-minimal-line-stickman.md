# STYLE-07 — Minimal Line / Stickman

## Visual identity
A minimal visual language built from simple line-drawn figures, sparse environments and high-contrast compositions. It merges the Minimal Line and Stickman Minimalis labels from the references because their core construction language is the same.

## Construction rules
- Lock head geometry, body/limb stroke, joint style, hand/foot treatment and figure proportions.
- Use a minimal palette, often monochrome with one accent color.
- Characters are simple line figures; distinguish roles through pose, small accessories or controlled accent details rather than realistic anatomy.
- Backgrounds remain sparse. Add only props or environmental lines needed to explain the action.
- Maintain consistent line caps, joins, stroke width and figure scale.

## Camera and motion
Use clean staging, line drawing/reveal, simple pose changes, walk cycles, held poses, pointer lines and minimal camera movement. Use negative space as an active compositional element. Avoid complex perspective unless the style board explicitly establishes it.

## Asset prompt requirements
Specify exact line color/weight, background, figure proportions, pose, accessory limits and whether the output is a transparent isolated figure or a complete scene. One character per character asset. Do not mix detailed documentary stickman or hand-drawn realism into this minimalist style.

## Avoid
Detailed anatomy, dense environments, multi-character sheets, gradients, inconsistent stroke weights and unnecessary decorative elements.
