# STYLE-10 — Explainer 3D

## Visual identity
A friendly, polished 3D educational animation style designed to make processes and concepts easy to understand. It uses stylized characters, clean environments, simplified mechanisms and strong visual hierarchy.

## Construction rules
- Choose a consistent character design system: proportions, facial detail, clothing, materials and expression range.
- Use smooth stylized geometry, soft but directional lighting and readable material separation.
- Environments are simplified and uncluttered; every object should support the explanation.
- Use a restrained palette with clear semantic colors for categories, flows or mechanisms.
- Technical objects must remain structurally understandable even when simplified.

## Camera and motion
Use clean medium/wide shots, gentle tracking, orbit and purposeful close-ups. Animate mechanisms in clear stages: establish, reveal, demonstrate, label and resolve. Combine 3D renders with editable Remotion typography, diagrams and callouts. Blender or another 3D tool handles modeling/rigging; Remotion handles composition, timing and graphics.

## Asset prompt requirements
Specify friendly educational 3D style, character proportions, material palette, environment simplicity, camera, lighting and exact action/state. Request clean negative space for text. Keep recurring characters and objects tied to approved reference images.

## Avoid
Overly childish proportions unless requested, noisy environments, photorealistic mismatch, random UI/text, excessive glossy materials and unmotivated motion.
