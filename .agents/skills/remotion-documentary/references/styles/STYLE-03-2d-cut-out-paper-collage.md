# 2D Cut-Out / Paper Collage — STYLE-03

## Purpose and visual identity
A tactile layered-paper visual language suited to history, inventions, archival storytelling and handcrafted explainers. The image should feel assembled from physical paper pieces, not flat vector UI.

## Character construction
Characters, if required, are built from distinct cut-paper shapes: head, hair/headwear, torso, sleeves, hands, legs and accessories. Limbs may use simple hinged segments. Keep silhouette and layer order consistent. Facial detail is minimal and may be printed/inked on the face layer. Characters are optional; do not invent them for object-led stories.

## Material and edge treatment
Use visible paper fibers, subtle grain, slightly irregular scissor-cut edges, layered thickness and soft contact shadows. Edge roughness must be controlled and repeatable. Avoid photorealistic skin or glossy plastic.

## Palette and rendering
Use a curated palette of paper stocks and ink colors with exact HEX values. Slight tonal variation may simulate paper batches but must remain within approved color limits. Shadows are soft, directional and consistent.

## Environments and objects
Construct locations and machines from stacked paper planes. Important mechanisms may use layered cutaway pieces, tabs, folded flaps and movable paper parts. Keep components distinct enough to animate independently.

## Layout and camera
Use front-facing or shallow three-quarter staging. Layer depth creates parallax. Keep the camera stable and avoid deep perspective that breaks the paper-theatre illusion.

## Animation behavior
Use hinged rotations, sliding layers, paper reveals, folds and parallax. Movement should feel like stop-motion or a carefully controlled paper puppet. Avoid elastic deformation and fluid morphing.

## Asset sheets
One character per character sheet; one object or coherent object family per object sheet. Include separate cut-paper component callouts only when they will be animated. Keep all cutouts separated with visible gaps and no overlap.

## Prompt constraints
Specify paper type, cut edge, layer thickness, grain, shadow direction, palette and camera. Exclude smooth vector finish, glossy 3D, unrelated characters and excessive distressed texture.
