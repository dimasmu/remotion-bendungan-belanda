# STYLE-05 — Comic Art

## Visual identity
A dynamic illustrated comic-book language with bold ink contours, graphic shadows, halftone textures, expressive poses and purposeful panel composition.

## Construction rules
- Lock outline weight hierarchy, ink color, halftone scale and palette.
- Use strong silhouettes, simplified anatomy and expressive readable gestures.
- Use selective speed lines, impact bursts, speech/thought bubbles and panel borders only when they support the story.
- Use limited cel shading and graphic shadow shapes; avoid soft airbrush rendering.
- Keep character model, costume, facial design and panel perspective consistent.

## Camera and motion
Compose like sequential comic panels. Use panel wipes, page turns, punch-in crops, impact holds, speed-line transitions and layered parallax. Reserve motion bursts for important narrative beats; do not animate every element simultaneously.

## Asset prompt requirements
Specify panel ratio, shot type, character identity, ink weight, halftone treatment, palette and speech-bubble policy. Prefer blank bubbles or no text; add exact dialogue and labels in Remotion. Request a clean focal hierarchy and safe space for typography.

## Avoid
Uncontrolled manga/anime drift, photorealism, random text, inconsistent ink thickness, overused explosions and crowded panels.
