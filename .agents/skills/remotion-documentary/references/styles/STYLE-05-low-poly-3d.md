## Low-Poly 3D — STYLE-05

## Purpose and visual identity
A stylized 3D language built from deliberate polygonal facets, simplified geometry and readable silhouettes. Suitable for engineering, machines, environments and object-led stories.

## Character construction
Characters are optional. If present, use simplified low-poly anatomy with locked head/body proportions, polygon density, facial style, clothing geometry and material assignments. Choose whether faces are faceted or smooth-shaded and keep that choice. Do not mix realistic humans with toy-like figures.

## Geometry and topology
Use intentional planar facets, clean silhouettes and consistent bevel policy. Detail should be concentrated on story-critical forms. Avoid random triangulation, messy topology and excessive micro-details.

## Palette and materials
Declare exact HEX/base-color values and material roughness/metallic intent. Prefer matte or satin materials; metallic surfaces only where justified. Avoid photorealistic textures unless approved as a hybrid.

## Environments and objects
Use modular low-poly buildings, terrain, vehicles and machinery. For mechanisms, create identifiable moving components and optional exploded/cutaway views. Keep scale relationships consistent.

## Lighting and camera
Use a stable key/fill/rim setup, soft but directional shadows and a defined camera lens/perspective. Isometric or three-quarter camera may be selected per project and locked.

## Animation behavior
Use rigid-body transforms, mechanical pivots and controlled camera movement. Avoid vertex morphing that changes the designed facets. Keep object pivots and scale consistent.

## Asset sheets
For recurring assets, create separate turntables/model sheets per character or object. Show front/side/back or orthographic/isometric views as useful. Sheet quantity and views depend on actual reuse and animation requirements.

## Prompt constraints
Specify polygon style, facet scale, topology feel, material roughness, lighting, camera and palette. Exclude random geometry, photorealistic skin, inconsistent polygon density and unapproved materials.
