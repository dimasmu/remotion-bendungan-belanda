# STYLE-08 — CGI Motion

## Visual identity
A cinematic computer-generated 3D look with dimensional objects, expressive lighting, depth, material response and deliberate camera work. It may be realistic or stylized, but the chosen rendering level must be locked for the whole project.

## Construction rules
- Define geometry/detail level, material roughness, reflections, lighting setup, color grade and render quality.
- Keep scale, proportions, object construction and material assignments consistent across shots.
- Use layered depth, atmospheric perspective and physically plausible contact shadows.
- Characters, if used, must have consistent model proportions, materials and facial style.
- Distinguish hero objects from supporting environment detail.

## Camera and motion
Plan lens, focal length, camera path, depth of field and motion blur deliberately. Use controlled dolly, orbit, crane, tracking and macro moves. Animate objects with clear physical intent. For complex 3D motion, render from Blender or another 3D tool and composite in Remotion; use Remotion for timing, layers, titles and transitions.

## Asset prompt requirements
Specify subject, material, environment, lens, camera angle, lighting direction, render style, output ratio and continuity references. Request separate passes/layers if compositing requires them. Do not generate factual labels or UI text.

## Avoid
Unmotivated camera movement, inconsistent reflections, floating objects, mixed render styles, excessive depth of field and overdone bloom.
