# STYLE-03 — Glow Sketch

## Visual identity
A dark, high-contrast visual world where subjects and diagrams are drawn with luminous sketch lines. The look combines hand-drawn contours with controlled neon emission and atmospheric darkness.

## Construction rules
- Lock a near-black background and a small neon palette with exact HEX values.
- Use bright line art as the primary visual information; fill areas only when necessary.
- Separate core strokes, secondary construction lines and glow bloom into distinct layers.
- Keep glow tight around the line; preserve sharp inner edges and readable silhouettes.
- Use a consistent line character: hand-drawn, technical, loose or geometric, chosen at project start.
- Characters and objects must share the same luminous outline logic.

## Camera and motion
Reveal strokes along paths, pulse selected nodes, trace mechanisms, draw connections and illuminate focal elements in sequence. Use slow camera pushes and parallax only when they clarify depth. Bloom should not obscure labels or intersections.

## Asset prompt requirements
Specify black-level background, line color, glow radius/intensity, line style, subject silhouette and camera angle. Request isolated layers or transparent exports when compositing requires them. Add text and precise diagrams in Remotion rather than relying on generated lettering.

## Avoid
Rainbow neon, blown-out bloom, glowing everything equally, gradients that destroy line contrast, realistic skin/materials mixed with sketch lines and cluttered backgrounds.
