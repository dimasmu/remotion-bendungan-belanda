# STYLE-03 — Glow Sketch

## Reference reading
The second reference shows a nearly black architectural/room-like space with a single simplified human figure drawn as a luminous outline. Warm yellow/gold and pale white lines define the person, floor plane, room edges and selected objects. The surrounding space stays almost entirely black. This is a sparse neon wire-sketch, not a fully glowing 3D scene.

## Visual signature
- Near-black background with large areas of negative space.
- Thin luminous contour lines describe the figure and a few architectural/environmental edges.
- Warm amber/yellow is the main glow; pale white or a restrained secondary hue may define selected edges.
- The subject is a simplified line figure, not a shaded realistic person.
- A subtle pool or trace of light on the floor anchors the figure in space.
- Only selected contours glow; the entire image must not become a neon poster.

## Geometry and line treatment
- Use clean but slightly hand-drawn contour paths, with consistent stroke hierarchy.
- Main subject outline is brightest; environmental construction lines are dimmer and thinner.
- Use minimal internal detail. Identity and pose should be readable from silhouette and gesture.
- Separate core line, soft outer bloom and optional floor reflection into layers.
- Keep joints and line connections clean; avoid disconnected limbs and ambiguous overlaps.

## Palette and lighting
- Sample the exact glow hue and intensity from the reference before setting HEX values.
- Default palette family: near-black background, warm gold/amber key lines, pale warm-white highlights and very dim charcoal secondary lines.
- Glow should have a crisp bright core and a narrow soft halo. Preserve black level and contrast.
- Avoid broad bloom that washes out the drawing.

## Camera and layout
- Use a wide dark stage with the figure positioned in a readable area.
- A simple room outline or floor grid may establish perspective, but must remain secondary.
- Camera is generally locked or uses a very slow push/pan. The feeling is an illuminated sketch appearing in darkness.
- Keep the frame sparse; one dominant subject/action per shot.

## Motion language
- Draw-on path reveals for body contours, room edges and object outlines.
- Small controlled pulses for the active object or point of interest.
- The figure can walk or gesture through sequential line poses; use clean pose changes rather than complex realistic anatomy.
- Floor glow may brighten under a footstep or active area.
- Use glow intensity as emphasis, not constant random flicker.

## Asset and prompt requirements
Specify black level, exact line colors, stroke width hierarchy, glow radius, subject pose, environment line density, camera and transparent-layer needs. Generate line-art subject and environment separately when independent motion is needed. Add all exact labels in Remotion.

## Do not
Do not use rainbow neon, thick cyberpunk signage, fully shaded characters, bright filled backgrounds, excessive bloom, random flicker or photorealistic objects mixed into the wire sketch.
