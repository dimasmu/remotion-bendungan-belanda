# STYLE-09 — Lego Style

## Visual identity
A miniature world constructed from interlocking toy bricks and minifigure-like characters. The visual appeal comes from modular geometry, recognizable studs, plastic materials and playful scale.

## Construction rules
- Use consistent brick dimensions, stud spacing, connection logic and modular proportions.
- Lock plastic material response, gloss level, edge bevels and palette.
- Characters use minifigure-inspired proportions and simplified faces; create original designs.
- Architecture, vehicles, props and terrain must look physically assembled from compatible pieces.
- Keep scale relationships consistent between figures, vehicles and buildings.

## Camera and motion
Use tabletop/diorama framing, miniature depth cues, controlled dolly/orbit and stop-motion-like object assembly when appropriate. Bricks should connect plausibly; moving mechanisms must preserve their construction. Remotion can animate camera, reveals and composited layers; Blender is suitable for complex brick assemblies and physics.

## Asset prompt requirements
Specify brick system, scale, stud visibility, plastic finish, palette, camera and assembly state. Request complete subjects with no cropped essential geometry. Use original characters and avoid logos or protected branded scenes.

## Avoid
Generic voxel cubes, inconsistent brick dimensions, realistic humans, impossible connections, mixed toy scales and uncontrolled plastic reflections.
