## Cinematic Photorealism — STYLE-10

## Purpose and visual identity
A realistic documentary image language focused on believable materials, lighting, scale and camera behavior. It is appropriate when the story benefits from photographic credibility.

## Character construction
Human characters are optional. If recurring people are needed, define identity continuity using non-sensitive fictional or consented references, wardrobe, age range as narratively appropriate, hair, facial structure and body proportions. Avoid unnecessary close-up identity detail. Keep costume, props and appearance consistent across shots.

## Realism and materials
Specify physically plausible material response, texture scale, wear, environmental interaction and imperfections. Avoid hyper-detailed noise or artificial sharpness. Historical reconstructions must be clearly treated as reconstructions where appropriate.

## Palette and lighting
Define a color grade, white balance, contrast, saturation, highlight roll-off and shadow treatment. Include exact palette anchors in HEX where practical, while allowing physically plausible tonal variation. Lock lighting continuity across connected shots.

## Environments and objects
Prioritize accurate scale, construction logic, material and weathering. Important objects require reference research and consistent geometry. Do not invent factual details that are not supported.

## Camera
Specify focal length range, camera height, framing, depth of field, shutter/motion feel and movement. Avoid impossible camera paths or inconsistent lens language.

## Animation behavior
Use realistic motion, inertia, environmental forces and physically plausible interactions. Maintain continuity in object positions, lighting, weather, wardrobe and camera geography.

## Asset sheets
For recurring subjects, create separate reference packs per character/object with front/side/back or coverage views as needed. These are continuity references, not necessarily production footage. Only request a sheet when reuse or consistency requires it.

## Prompt constraints
Specify realism level, material references, camera, lighting, grade and factual anchors. Exclude illustration-style linework, fantasy embellishments, inconsistent wardrobe and unsupported historical claims.
